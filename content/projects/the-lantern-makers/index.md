## Aims

#### Investigate

the feasibility and challenges of implementing 2D animation in immersive video environments.

#### Develop

a tailored workflow and guidelines for 2D animation hobbyists.

---

## Short Film

![Some of the explored storylines. Due to time constraints and my own artistic ability, the Lantern-Making Storyline was finalised.](image-1.webp)

> A young boy, estranged from his passion for lantern-making, rediscovers it with his father’s support. Set in the Japanese countryside, the story explores themes of self-discovery, creative fulfillment, and embracing imperfections in this coming-of-age tale.
>

## The Hard Part

![A lot of back and forth for getting the numbers correct in Premier Pro](image-6.webp)

![Background made using equirectangular grid guides. For more information regarding panoramic drawing, I recommend D. M. Swart’s [article](https://dmswart.com/2016/06/28/drawing-a-panorama/)](scene-1-20260403151215.mp4)

![If you want 360 video use the one on the left, for 180 just crop out the sides as shown on right.](image-7.webp)

![This workflow takes time initially but improves alignment and proportions. With practice, artists can adapt quickly to panoramic drawing grids, and can skip the 3D part all together](image-8.webp)
